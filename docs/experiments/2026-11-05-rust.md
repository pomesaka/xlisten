# xlisten in Rust (2026-11-05)

On the same Mac as [the first runs](2026-11-05-first-runs.md): whisper.cpp 1.8.3 through `whisper-rs` 0.16.0 (Metal), speech.cpp v0.10.1 through its C API (Metal), large-v3-turbo-q5_0. Each was run once.

## Diarization after the meeting

The sound of the apps of a Slack huddle, recorded with catap from 11:12 to its end: 1281.23 s, through `speech diarize nemotron-3-diarization` (offline): 7.109 s, RTFx 180, 422 segments of 5 speakers (364, 39, 16, 2 and 1 of them). Matched to the 194 `others` lines by time, 160 went to one speaker, 21 to a second, 11 to a third, 1 to a fourth; in the talk about the name shown at the top right, the one who asked and the one who had fixed it came out apart, line by line. The start of the recording was taken from the time the file was last written less its length, so the lines may be off by some hundred ms.

## Both inputs, the others told apart as they speak

55 s, `say` playing a talk through the monitor's speakers: Kyoko (Japanese) three times, Daniel (English) twice, Samantha (English) once, with a second between them.

| Voice | Line | `speaker` |
|---|---|---|
| Kyoko | それでは定例を始めます。今日は3つ議題があります。 | A |
| Daniel | ご視聴ありがとうございました (said in English: "Thanks. First, the release is scheduled for next Tuesday.") | B |
| Kyoko | わかりました。テストの状況はどうですか。 | A |
| Samantha | The tests are mostly green, but two cases still fail. | A |
| Daniel | I will look into those failures this afternoon | B |
| Kyoko | ありがとうございます。では次の議題に移ります。 | A |

Kyoko and Daniel were told apart each time; Samantha was taken for Kyoko. Not run: a meeting of Japanese voices told apart as they speak.

`xlisten latency`: `others` 6 lines, median 1.60 s, max 2.42 s (whisper 0.96 to 0.99 s an utterance; the line waits for the diarization to settle its end); `me` 1 line, 2.15 s. RE-USE took 0.31 to 0.35 of the length of each utterance of the microphone.

## Order of the inputs

With the tap's aggregate device started first, `AudioOutputUnitStart` of the microphone (cpal) waited in Core Audio and never returned: the process stayed in `HALB_IOThread::StartAndWaitForState` and SIGTERM did not end it. With the microphone first, both started, the two times it was run on 2026-11-05.

## Stopping

SIGTERM to the PID of `xlisten.pid` while both inputs ran: the `ended` line was written, `xlisten.pid` removed, and the process ended within 4 s.
